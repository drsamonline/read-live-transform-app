# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First published version of the **Read. Live. Transform.** app.
- The whole book — 74 chapters in 12 parts — carried inside one offline file.
- **Today:** one chapter a day as a three-step quest (Read · Live · Transform).
- **Read:** the full book, with questions that save as you type.
- **Practice:** the seven-day practice and "My moves".
- **You:** level and rank, stats, ten achievements, export and reset.
- Progression: ranks E–S, XP, day streak, level-up panel.
- Offline PWA: service worker, installable, no dependencies, ~292 KB.
