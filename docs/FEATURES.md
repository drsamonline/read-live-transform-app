# Features

## Today

One chapter a day, chosen by the date, so the book unfolds over 74 days. The
verse is shown, then the day's **quest** — three steps:

1. **Read** — the verse, slowly
2. **Live** — one practice, today
3. **Transform** — keep the change

One button at the bottom advances you to the next step, so there is never a wall
of choices. Completing all three completes the quest.

## Read

The whole book: the front matter, the twelve parts, and all seventy-four
chapters. Tap a chapter for the reader:

- the verse card (reference + Pickthall wording),
- the explanation,
- the *read carefully* note,
- the practice,
- the questions — write your answers and they save as you type,
- the takeaway.

## Practice

- **A seven-day practice** — the book's own seven steps, as a checklist.
- **My moves** — every practice you keep from a chapter. Tap "done today" each
  day; the count of days kept is shown.

## You

- Your **level and rank** in a system panel.
- **Stats:** chapters read, moves kept, days kept, day streak.
- **Ten achievements**, from *First Verse* to *Whole Book* and *Month Streak*.
- The book's front and back matter (Author's Note, How to Use, Before You Begin,
  A Note on the Text, the Seven-Day Practice, Glossary, About the Author).
- **Copy my practice** — export your moves and notes as text.
- **Reset my data** — clear everything on the device.

## Progression

Ranks run **E → S**, each with a title:

| Rank | Level | Title |
|---|---|---|
| E | 1–3 | Seeker |
| D | 4–6 | Student |
| C | 7–9 | Practitioner |
| B | 10–12 | Steady |
| A | 13–15 | Devoted |
| S | 16+ | Transformed |

XP is earned by doing:

| Action | XP |
|---|---|
| Read a chapter | +10 |
| Keep a practice | +15 |
| Mark a practice done (per day) | +5 |
| Each day of the seven-day practice | +10 |
| Finish the seven-day practice | +60 |
| Complete a day's quest | +25 |

Crossing a threshold opens a **level-up** panel with your new rank. A day streak
counts the days you return.
